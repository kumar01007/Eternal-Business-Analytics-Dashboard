


# Eternal Business Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI analytics dashboard** designed to analyze business performance across Eternal's key consumer-facing verticals — **Zomato, Blinkit, and District**.

The project focuses on tracking business KPIs, understanding trends across different business verticals, and presenting data-driven insights through an interactive multi-page dashboard.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Eternal operates across multiple consumer businesses including food delivery, quick commerce, and entertainment.

This project brings these verticals into a unified Power BI dashboard to provide an overview of business performance while allowing users to explore individual verticals.

The dashboard contains four major views:

- **Eternal Overview**
- **Zomato Analytics**
- **Blinkit Analytics**
- **District Analytics**

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Track important business KPIs
- Analyze order, sales, and booking trends
- Identify high-performing categories
- Understand customer and product trends
- Compare performance across business verticals
- Build an interactive reporting solution for business decision-making
- Generate actionable insights from business data

## 📊 Dashboard Structure

### 1. Eternal Overview

The main dashboard provides a consolidated view of business performance across the different verticals.

Key areas include:

- Overall business KPIs
- Revenue / sales performance
- Orders / transactions
- Customer trends
- Vertical-level performance
- Business growth trends

---

### 2. Zomato Analytics

The Zomato view focuses on food delivery and restaurant-related analytics.

Key areas include:

- Order trends
- Restaurant performance
- Customer preferences
- Restaurant ratings
- Food categories
- Online ordering trends

---

### 3. Blinkit Analytics

The Blinkit view focuses on quick-commerce and grocery business performance.

Key areas include:

- Sales performance
- Product categories
- Outlet performance
- Consumer purchasing patterns
- Category-level trends

---

### 4. District Analytics

The District view focuses on entertainment and booking-related performance.

Key areas include:

- Booking trends
- Category performance
- Revenue / transaction trends
- Movies
- Events
- Dining categories

## 📈 Key KPIs

The dashboard focuses on business metrics available in the underlying datasets, including:

- Total Orders / Transactions
- Total Revenue / Sales
- Total Customers / Users
- Average Order Value
- Average Rating
- Category Performance
- Booking Performance
- Growth Trends

## 💡 Business Questions

The dashboard is designed to help answer questions such as:

* Which business vertical is performing strongest?
* Which categories contribute most to sales or transactions?
* How are orders and bookings changing over time?
* Which products, restaurants, or categories have stronger performance?
* What customer preferences can be identified from the available data?
* Which business areas require further investigation?
* How can business teams use KPI trends to support decision-making?

---

## 🎯 Business Recommendations

The analysis can support business decisions around:

### 1. Category Optimization

Identify high-performing and underperforming categories to improve marketing, product placement, and inventory decisions.

### 2. Customer Engagement

Analyze customer behavior patterns to identify opportunities for improving engagement and repeat usage.

### 3. Performance Monitoring

Monitor important KPIs to identify sudden changes in orders, sales, ratings, or bookings and investigate potential causes.

### 4. Vertical-Level Strategy

Compare performance across Zomato, Blinkit, and District to identify areas with stronger business opportunities.
